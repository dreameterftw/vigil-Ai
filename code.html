Your questions, answered

Everything you need to know about continuous risk, DealLock contracts, and capital allocation.

How does VIGIL calculate the Cyber Risk Index?
VIGIL continuously weighs 5 critical dimensions: Identity Risk (counterparty GSTIN, PAN, and active bank registrations), Transaction Risk (outflow-to-liquidity spikes), Communication Risk (heuristic scanning of email/invoices for phishing vectors), Contract Risk (absence of cryptographic terms), and Historical Counterparty Discipline. Each factor is scored from 0-20 to generate a 0-100 aggregate score.
What is DealLock™ and how does the Polygon contract work?
DealLock converts agreed B2B milestones, delivery timelines, and statutory penalty terms into a cryptographic SHA-256 hash published directly to the Polygon Amoy testnet. This creates an unforgeable, independently verifiable timestamp that legally proves agreed commercial terms in case of delayed payment or fraudulent alteration.
Does VIGIL Invest give regulated financial advice?
No. VIGIL Invest is an educational, algorithmic treasury decision-support tool. It calculates recommended cash safety buffers (e.g., 90-day payroll and supplier burn) before showing what portion of your working capital might be safely deployed into liquid, low-risk instruments. We do not execute trades or hold client deposits.
How does the MSME Samadhaan complaint draft work?
If an invoice crosses the statutory 45-day threshold, VIGIL automatically compiles the purchase order, delivery receipts, GST e-invoices, and DealLock timestamps into the exact legal draft format mandated by the Micro and Small Enterprise Facilitation Council (MSEFC).
What data is required to verify a counterparty on Watchtower?
You only need the counterparty’s 15-digit GSTIN, entity PAN, or registered IFSC bank branch. Watchtower queries public registry nodes and return-filing cadences in real-time to alert you of dormant shells or mismatched names before payments leave your account.
